# Frog Hop Sums

A maths game for the iPad: adding and taking away, with a frog that hops along a number line to help.

**Up to 20 (for the test):** adding, taking away, missing numbers, a mix, and a 10-sum practice test with no helper.

**Bigger sums (like the homework):** word sums ("The total of 43 and 18 is ☐"), missing numbers up to 2000 ("68 + ☐ = 100"), adding three numbers, and story sums. The frog shows the "friendly jumps" method: count up to the next ten, then the next hundred, then the rest.

How it works:
- Rounds are 10 sums. Getting a sum right first time earns a star, and finishing a round earns a sticker (gold for 10 out of 10).
- First wrong answer: "have another go". Second wrong answer: the frog shows the jumps and says the answer.
- Sums she gets wrong are remembered on the iPad and come back in later rounds until she gets them right.

## Putting it on the iPad

It's a single file, `index.html`, with no build step. Host it anywhere (for example GitHub Pages), open it in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it opens full-screen like an app.
